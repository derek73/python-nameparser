# T9 — a slash written for a title's full stop

## Standing

- A listed title opening the name, or the given part after a family comma, written with a slash in place of its full stop reads as that title, the slash a separator in no field, in each of its four writings (`د/ أحمد`, `د/أحمد`, `د / أحمد`, `د /أحمد`; `Dr/ Jane Smith`), any script alike; a title that is also a suffix, a given name or a saint particle is none, a title listed with a slash of its own is the title whole, and the name after the slash must be one name word in T8's shape that no title or suffix reading claims outright, a title or suffix that is also a given name read as after the title's full stop (`Dr/ Dean Smith` → given 'Dean') — 2026-11-20, #1149.
- The part before a family comma is not read, a slash there joining two surnames (`JURIST/KOVAC, DANIEL/KAREN`); the comma's own decision is asked first, so a part after it that C1 reads as post-nominals leaves the head read (`Dr/ Jane Smith, Jr.`) — 2026-11-20, #1149.

Elsewhere: a slash joining credentials, decisions/S3; a slash standing alone after the first comma, decisions/C10; how the slash's question was measured before it was built, decisions/script-properties' 2026-11-19 #1149 entry.
