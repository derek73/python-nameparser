## Standing

- C-i: whether a word is marked ambiguous turns on whether it is borne as an ordinary name in the position the vocabulary claim acts on, a bearer elsewhere not counting (`Bimal De` leaves `de` never-given, the surname being trailing), and under uncertainty it is marked ambiguous; for the particles being borne there earns the marking, while for the suffix acronyms it only opens the question, which relative frequency answers by removal, marking or leaving the word (decisions/suffix-acronym-collisions) — 2026-08-16 #348/#360/#342/#385; 2026-08-17.
- C-i applies to particles, suffix acronyms and titles alike, with one exception for titles alone: words measured as borne in the leading position at small incidence ship as titles (`herra`, `batoni`, `hrabia`, `kreivi`), and the exception reaches no other set or word without its own counts — 2026-08-16 #348/#360/#342/#385; 2026-10-04 #606.
- The gap between the ambiguous shares of the particles and of the suffix acronyms is base rate, not inconsistency: most particles are short words that double as names and most credential acronyms are not, so the shares are not to be harmonized — 2026-08-16 #348/#360/#342/#385.
- C-ii: where two sets claim a word and neither reading is a name, precedence is a frequency judgement recorded per word; `vd`, a never-given particle and a credential acronym, takes the Dutch van der reading where a family is named (`Berg, Jan vd` → family 'vd Berg') — 2026-08-16 #348/#360/#342/#385.
