# O9 — words in capitals as the family

## Standing

- The default reading of a name with words in capitals does not change: `DUPONT Mary` keeps given `DUPONT` — 2026-11-11 #659.
- Reading the capitals as the family is an opt-in `Policy` switch, `capitals_as_family`, off by default; no locale pack sets it; the decision also has the group suggester turn it on for a list that writes its families in capitals consistently, which a later PR implements — 2026-11-11 #659.
- The run of capitals words in a mixed-case name is the family wherever it stands, outranking the credential and title readings, the other words keeping their written order — 2026-11-11 #659.
- A capitals word holds two or more letters in capitals and no full stop, slash or digit; runs join across particles and conjunctions, a lowercase particle right before the run joins it, a run of particles alone or a lone roman numeral is none, the last of two runs is the family, and only the part before the first comma is read — 2026-11-12 #659.
- A listed credential or numeral ending the name peels off before a run is chosen, wherever a capitals word that is no particle is left — 2026-11-12 #659, the FOURTH REVIEW.
- A word the patronymic rule in force reads as its marker is no capitals word where another capitals word that is no particle, conjunction or marker is left to be the family — 2026-11-12 #659, the FIFTH REVIEW.
- A conjunction is no word the peel must leave — 2026-11-12 #659, the FIFTH REVIEW.
- The switch cannot be set with `unlisted_caps_suffixes=CapsSuffixes.EVERYWHERE`: construction raises — 2026-11-11 #659.
- A report on a word of the run, a `given-or-family` report and a `particle-or-given` report are dropped; a title or suffix fork on any other word stands under every order, and the rule reports nothing of its own — 2026-11-12 #659, the SECOND FIX REVIEW.
- The given name is the first unit holding a word that may be one: a conjunction or particle written in lowercase is a joining word, a lowercase particle staying with the word after it; a particle written with a capital may be the given name — 2026-11-12 #659, the FIX REVIEW.
- Mixed case is read over the words no run took, after the peel: a word other than a title, a suffix, or a particle or conjunction written in lowercase holds a lowercase letter — 2026-11-12 #659, the SECOND FIX REVIEW.
- An `order` report, and a report on a word the peel took, are dropped where the rule acts — 2026-11-12 #659, the THIRD REVIEW.

Open: #659 (a bare `DR` in capitals at the head reads as the family under the switch, `DR Anna Berg`; whether a title that is never a surname should stand is undecided).
