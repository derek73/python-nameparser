# deviates-registry — packs stay pure data (option C)

## Standing

- A locale pack declares its deviations from the defaults as a module-level `DEVIATES` callable, acceptance metadata kept off `Locale`, whose value equality and pickling a callable field would break; `test_registry_is_the_pack_contract` fails unless every registered pack ships `DEVIATES` and a rotator list, which is what lets mechanisms.md#LOCALE-PACKS-PURE-DATA promise packs no code paths of their own. A field on `Locale` is to be revisited only if a runtime consumer of the declaration, such as an `explain()`, appears, and none has — 2026-07-18. A vocabulary pack's deviations are derivable instead, from the reading index (`_reading_index.changed_words`), which tests/v2/test_locales.py's choice gate reads; the gate ors the derived surface into every registered pack with `languages`, so a hand `DEVIATES` declares only what a fragment or a patch beside a choice changes; the contract test refuses a `DEVIATES` on a pack that is nothing but a choice of languages (`locales.NE`, the first) and requires one of every other pack — 2026-10-29 #753, phase 3b; 2026-10-30 #753, phase 3d.

Open: none in this file.

## Log

- 2026-07-18 (d4aaafa; the DEVIATES design note) — a `deviates` field ON Locale was rejected: callables break value equality and pickle-by-reference, and DEVIATES is acceptance metadata, not runtime behavior (revisit only if a runtime consumer like explain() appears). The REGISTRY became the contract instead: test_registry_is_the_pack_contract fails structurally unless every registered pack ships DEVIATES and its rotator list. This is why LOCALE-PACKS-PURE-DATA can promise "no code paths of their own" and mean it.
- 2026-10-29 #753, phase 3b — A VOCABULARY PACK'S DEVIATIONS ARE DERIVED, NOT DECLARED. `Locale.languages` selects among the default's readings, so the words a choice can move are computable: `_reading_index.changed_words(chosen)` is every word joining or leaving a compiled constant under the choice, and tests/v2/test_locales.py's `test_non_interference_each_choice` requires the default's reading for every corpus name holding none of them, with a negative control (`test_the_choice_gate_fails_when_its_surface_misses_a_word`). No pack ships in 3b, so the gate runs over choices of the files' tags; the registry contract test still asks every REGISTERED pack for `DEVIATES` and a rotator list, and learns to read a derived surface for a pack with `languages` in the PR that registers the first one (3d). The policy and segmentation packs (JA, RU, TR_AZ, ZH) keep theirs, decisions/vocabulary-model's 2026-10-27 #753 entry, item 7.
- 2026-10-30 #753, phase 3d — THE CONTRACT READS A DERIVED SURFACE. `locales.NE` registers the first pack whose content is a choice of languages and nothing else (`Locale.lexicon` empty, `Locale.policy` the empty patch). tests/v2/test_locales.py's `_deviates(code)` gives the gate, the rotator test and the combined gate every pack's predicate: the surface derived from its `languages` through `changed_words` and the 3b choice gate's `_surface_deviates`, or'd with its `DEVIATES`, so a pack with a fragment or a patch beside a choice declares only that part (`test_a_pack_with_a_choice_and_a_patch_declares_only_the_patch`); `test_registry_is_the_pack_contract` refuses a `DEVIATES` on a pack that is nothing but its choice, so a hand copy cannot drift beside the derived one, and still requires one of every other pack. Its rotator list stays required, as the gate's positive side. decisions/vocabulary-model's 2026-10-30 phase 3d entry, item 2.
