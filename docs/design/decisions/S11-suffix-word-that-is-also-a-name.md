# S11 — a suffix word that is also a name

## Standing

- A generation word with a `given` or `family` reading is gated as a bare ambiguous acronym is: the generation where the name has words to spare, the name otherwise, either reading reported (`Pedro Alves Neto` → suffix 'Neto', `Ana Neto` → family 'Neto') — 2026-10-25 #752.
- The Brazilian agnomens (`filho`, `filha`, `neto`, `neta`, `netto`, `sobrinho`, `sobrinha`) are generation readings of the `agnomen` kind, gated by the count alone, their writing never leaning; `fils` is a `generation` reading whose lower case in a mixed-case name is the generation and whose Title case leans to the name (`Marie Fils` → family 'Fils') — 2026-10-25 #752.
- A suffix word that is also a name makes no chunk claim inside a word with interior periods, which reads by its dotted shape as it did before the word was listed: `Pedro M.Filho` keeps family 'M.Filho', and `Pedro Alves M.Filho` reads suffix 'M.Filho' as on master — 2026-10-25 #752.

- Only name words in front of a generation word that is also a name count toward its words to spare, and a suffix word there is no company for it: `Pedro Junior Filho` and `Pedro VI Filho` keep family 'Filho' — 2026-10-25 (Derek), #890.

- The floor holds before a generation word's case: a lower-case `fils` in a mixed-case name is the generation only where the name keeps a family word, so `Dumas fils` and `Pedro Junior fils` read family 'fils' — 2026-10-25 (Derek), #890, THE FLOOR.

Open: none in this file; #843 keeps the agnomens' abbreviations (`FH`, `FO`, `NT`, `SB`).
