# O4 — positional assignment and declared order

## Standing

- Romanized Chinese order is declared, not detected, pinyin carrying no signal and diaspora making locale no guide: a caller whose data is family-first passes `Policy(name_order=FAMILY_FIRST)` — 2026-08-07 #83.
- There are three order constants because Vietnamese needs the third: `Nguyễn Thị Minh Khai` reads given 'Khai', middle 'Thị Minh', family 'Nguyễn' under FAMILY_FIRST_GIVEN_LAST, where FAMILY_FIRST strands given 'Thị' — 2026-08-07 #146.
- Free-form order tuples are declined (#270's draft shape; the decline is the log's undated Declined line, which no dated entry records): a tuple of strings raises `name_order elements must be Role members` (rules/D2) and a two-role tuple `name_order must be one of the exported orders` — 2026-08-07 #146.
- `middle_as_family` is declined as the way to drop Vietnamese middles, giving family 'Thị Minh Nguyễn' for `Nguyễn Thị Minh Khai` under FAMILY_FIRST_GIVEN_LAST; `given_names` is the view that stays correct — 2026-08-07 #146.
- One name word followed by nothing but initials reports `order`, the reserved kind, with no alternative, under the given-first order, default or declared, with no family comma and `initials_as_family` off; capitals written as one word with no full stops (`Armitage JO`) do not count as initials there — 2026-11-11 #1254.

  - The kind: `order`, reserved since 2.0 for "the name's field order itself is uncertain", which this is; a new kind was the other choice, and the reserved one adds none to the public enum.
  - Its reach: one name word, then letters in rules/T5's shape (spaced, or joined by full stops), ending the name words; under the given-first order, read by declaration or by default alike, since nothing tells the two apart; not after a family comma, not under `initials_as_family`, not when the word in front is itself a run of initials (rules/T7), holds no letter, is a particle (whose own fork reports it) or is in a script that writes none; and not where a letter is no initial to T5: hyphenated (`J.-P.`), a transliteration digraph (`Yu.`, rules/H2), a one-case letter the vocabulary does not mark (rules/P8's `Y`), or a Devanagari letter written with a vowel sign (`बि.क.`; 1,407 nepal-candidates names of the shape report nothing, while all 1,580 of the new reports there are Latin, a count the #1348 review took; #1273). The detail says the word is the only name word before the initials, since a post-nominal may follow them (`PHUOC T DO`).
