# P12 — a bound given name and the word that completes it

## Standing

- A bound given-name word past the first name word joins the word after it where its reading lists that word in `completed_by`, the pair one name unit that position places: `طارق عبد العزيز` gives family 'عبد العزيز' — 2026-11-04 #694.
- For Arabic-script `عبد` the license is a closed list on the bound word's reading, not a shape of the word after it: `عبد` lists 217 spellings of 139 words, the 97 one-word names of God, `الله`, 27 other divine epithets and 14 devotional completions, each with its hamza-less and Persian spellings, and `الله` with the Urdu heh too — 2026-11-04 #694.
- The Latin `abd`, `abdel`, `abdal` and `abou` are written `completes_any`: past the first name word each joins any plain name word after it, one the vocabulary reads no other way, the pair placed by position, the family where it ends the name and one middle name inside it (`Tariq Abdel Aziz` → family 'Abdel Aziz') — 2026-11-19 #1464, reversing 2026-11-04 #694's decline.
- `abdul` is written `long_names_only` beside it, joining only in a name of four name words or more, counted part by part before a maiden clause: a word with no letter and each part's leading title run (a listed title, a title phrase H9 reads with the word its connective joins, a dotted title, a title by H2's shape) counting as none, and after the pair only a plain word, a bound given name or a pair of initials in a script with case up to the next comma, a numeral `VI` to `X` written other than in Title case counting as none (`Tariq Abdul Aziz` → middle 'Abdul'; `Nadia Ali Abdul Rahim` → family 'Abdul Rahim'): the writers of a three-word `X Abdul Y` split it both ways by population — 2026-11-19 #1464.
- `abu` keeps its particle and teknonymic readings and no `completes_any`; Arabic-script `عبد` keeps its list — 2026-11-19 #1464.
- A pair P12 joined counts as one family word for rules/O6 (two family names): under `two_family_names` `سعيد حسن عبد الله` gives family 'حسن عبد الله'; P5's pair at the start and a pair a particle chain took count as two — 2026-11-04 #694; a Latin pair the join makes past the first name word counts as one, as the Arabic-script pair does (`Ana abdul Rahman López` under `two_family_names` gives family 'abdul Rahman López'), where one at the start still counts as two — 2026-11-19 #1464.

Open: a Malay choice could turn `abdul`'s three-word join on, its Malaysian writers filing the pair as the family 183 times to 2 in ORCID; the Malay choice is #1179's question (2026-11-19 #1464).

Open: the second elements that join the word BEFORE them (`الدين`, `الله`, `الغنى` in `جميل عز الدين`, `هالة شكر الله`, `عائشة ذو الغنى`), the other three Arabic-script names `wikidata_native_agreement.json` held on the shape, want a mechanism of their own and are not decided here.
