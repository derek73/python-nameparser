# P12 — a bound given name and the word that completes it

## Standing

- A bound given-name word past the first name word joins the word after it where its reading lists that word in `completed_by`, the pair one name unit that position places: `طارق عبد العزيز` gives family 'عبد العزيز' — 2026-11-04 #694.
- For Arabic-script `عبد` the license is a closed list on the bound word's reading, not a shape of the word after it: `عبد` lists 239 spellings of 139 words on 2026-11-20 (217 on 2026-11-04; `readings_of('عبد')`'s `completed_by`), the 97 one-word names of God, `الله`, 27 other divine epithets and 14 devotional completions, each with its hamza-less and Persian spellings, and `الله` with the Urdu heh too — 2026-11-04 #694; each word ending in `ي` with its `ى` spelling too, and `المولى` with its `ي` spelling, so `سامر عبد القوى` gives family 'عبد القوى' — 2026-11-20 #1337.
- The Latin `abd`, `abdel`, `abdal` and `abou` are written `completes_any`: past the first name word each joins any plain name word after it, one the vocabulary reads no other way, the pair placed by position, the family where it ends the name and one middle name inside it (`Tariq Abdel Aziz` → family 'Abdel Aziz') — 2026-11-19 #1464, reversing 2026-11-04 #694's decline.
- `abdul` is written `long_names_only` beside it, joining only in a name the parse reads as four name words or more: the words it reads as given, middle and family names that hold a letter, the two parts of a family comma counted together, so a title, a suffix, a nickname, a maiden name and a lone mark are no name words and a word the parse reads as a name is one whatever else its vocabulary says (`Tariq Abdul Aziz` → middle 'Abdul'; `Nadia Ali Abdul Rahim` → family 'Abdul Rahim'): the writers of a three-word `X Abdul Y` split it both ways by population — 2026-11-19 #1464; counted by the parse rather than by classify since 2026-11-22 #1531.
- `abu` keeps its particle and teknonymic readings and no `completes_any`; Arabic-script `عبد` keeps its list — 2026-11-19 #1464.
- A pair is read from its second word too: a `bound_second` reading (Arabic-script `الدين` and `الله`, Persian `الدین` and `اله`, Urdu `اللہ`) joins the plain word before it, one carrying no vocabulary and holding a letter, at the first name word too, the pair placed by position, under the default order the given name where it opens the name and the family where it ends it (`نور الدين سامر حداد` → given 'نور الدين'; `سامر عز الدين` → family 'عز الدين'); P5's join of a bound word opening the name wins over a pair read from the word after it (`أبو نور الدين سامر` → given 'أبو نور'); `الغنى` and `الفقار` are written `completes = ["ذو"]`, joining behind `ذو` alone; where the pair holds every name word the name is read as having it is split back into two names, as P5's reserve keeps a bound word's, save after a family comma and behind a given-name title, where P5 joins too; the pair is one unit wherever UNIT-PARTITION counts units (P1's fold, rules/O6's take and its division) — 2026-11-19 #1072.
- A pair P12 joined counts as one family word for rules/O6 (two family names): under `two_family_names` `سعيد حسن عبد الله` gives family 'حسن عبد الله'; P5's pair at the start and a pair a particle chain took count as two — 2026-11-04 #694; a Latin pair the join makes past the first name word counts as one, as the Arabic-script pair does (`Ana abdul Rahman López` under `two_family_names` gives family 'abdul Rahman López'), where one at the start still counts as two — 2026-11-19 #1464.

Open: `locales.MS` (shipped by #1179; no issue filed for this) could turn `abdul`'s three-word join on, its Malaysian writers filing the pair as the family 183 times to 2 in ORCID; as shipped it reads `abdul` as the default does, and a strict choice of it reads no Latin bound word at all, `ar.toml` not being chosen (2026-11-19 #1464).

Open: the Latin spellings of a pair read from its second word stay out (2026-11-19 #1072): `Uddin`, the commonest, is a surname alone in South Asian names (PSC 25 records fixed and 432 broken, ORCID's Bangladesh 15 and 99, the prototype scored against the boxes on 2026-11-19), and without the `u-` forms (`Eddine`, `Eldin`, `Aldeen` and the like, and `Allah` behind a word other than `Abd`) ORCID gains 155 and loses none, but PSC 16 and 11 and PatentsView 6 and 8.

Open: `آية الله` (Ayatollah) is no title (2026-11-19 #1072). Today's vocabulary cannot make it one: a title phrase's partner must be a title word, `الله` cannot take a title reading without breaking the pairs read from it (`ليلى شكر الله`, which a title-tagged second word does not join; `عبد الله` still joins forward), and `آية` alone is the given name Aya. Reading it as a title would need the title phrase test to accept a partner with no title reading. The shape is 16 spaced spellings in JRC-Names (`آية`/`اية الله` 11, Persian `آیت الله` 5) and 7 written joined with a ZWNJ, which the join does not touch; none in ORCID or the parliaments.
