# P12 — a bound given name and the word that completes it

## Standing

- A bound given-name word past the first name word joins the word after it where its reading lists that word in `completed_by`, the pair one name unit that position places: `طارق عبد العزيز` gives family 'عبد العزيز' — 2026-11-04 #694.
- The license is a closed list on the bound word's reading, not a shape of the word after it: Arabic-script `عبد` lists 216 spellings: the 97 one-word names of God, `الله` and 28 other divine epithets, and 14 devotional completions found after it in the corpora, each with its hamza-less and Persian spellings, and `الله` with the Urdu heh too — 2026-11-04 #694.
- The Latin `abdul`, `abdel` and `abdal` list no word, so a non-leading one stays apart (`Tariq Abdul Aziz` → middle 'Abdul') — 2026-11-04 #694.
- The pair counts as one family word for rules/O6 (two family names): under `two_family_names` `سعيد حسن عبد الله` gives family 'حسن عبد الله' — 2026-11-04 #694.

Open: the second elements that join the word BEFORE them (`الدين`, `الله`, `الغنى` in `جميل عز الدين`, `هالة شكر الله`, `عائشة ذو الغنى`), the other three Arabic-script names `wikidata_native_agreement.json` held on the shape, want a mechanism of their own and are not decided here.
