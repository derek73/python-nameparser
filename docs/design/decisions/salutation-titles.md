# salutation-titles — salutations, offices and ranks for the languages TITLES had none for (2026-10-04, #606)

Closes #606 (#662 later added owura and bapak, its bullet below, and #847 cllr). No parser code moves: 41 words join TITLES and one, knt (Knight, beside kt), joins SUFFIX_ACRONYMS with a `Knt` case mask. The class is decided once — a word that addresses or ranks a person and is written in FRONT of the name — and each word is then argued on #vocabulary-collisions C-i, the position test, because TITLES has no ambiguous subset and the only two answers available are ship and do not ship. The per-word lists, grouped by language, are the titles carrying `issue = 606` (`uv run python tools/vocab/query.py --issue 606 --kind title`; one line per file a word is in; the #606 block of nameparser/config/titles.py until 2026-10-18, then TOML comments in nameparser/vocab/und.toml, and since 2026-10-19 (#750) the salutations' entries in their language files, and the offices', the noble Counts' and knight's too since the second part of #750 the same day).

## Standing

- A word that addresses or ranks a person and is written in FRONT of the name ships as a title unless it is borne as an ordinary name in the leading position, the position test of vocabulary-collisions' C-i, TITLES having no ambiguous subset to mark a collision in, so the only answers are ship and do not ship — 2026-10-04 #606.
- One exception to C-i, for TITLES alone: a word borne as a name only RARELY as the first word of one ships on the precedent of `graf` (`herra`, `batoni`, `hrabia`, `kreivi` and the rest of the graf class, each with its counts on its title reading's `note`), and no word in the particle or suffix sets, and no later title, takes it without its own counts — 2026-10-04 #606.
- The cost falls on a name that BEGINS with such a word: `Greve Anna` reads title 'Greve', family 'Anna' and `Herra Wijaya` title 'Herra', family 'Wijaya'; a declared `FAMILY_FIRST` does not help (`Hrabia Anna` reads title 'Hrabia' under it); the family comma keeps the word as the family where no name word stands behind it (`Hrabia, Anna` reads given 'Anna', family 'Hrabia', and so does `Hrabia Jr., Anna`), while a name word behind it makes it the title by H6 (`Hrabia Nowak, Anna` reads title 'Hrabia', family 'Nowak'); a name that ends in the word keeps it (`Gladys Knight`, `Knight, Gladys` read family 'Knight'), unless a period follows it (`Gladys Knight.` reads title 'Knight.', family 'Gladys', by H5, accepted rather than prevented) — 2026-10-04 #606.
- Words judged a COMMON leading-position name stay out (`thiru`, `puan`, `pan`, `rodina`, and `ông` and its pair `bà`), and words with no evidence either way are not shipped (`gróf`, `cik`, `paron`, `tikin`), each an `excluded` entry in its language's file, and Latin `janab`, whose exclusion was removed on 2026-11-03 with no evidence to refuse it by either (decisions/indic-honorifics, #345) (`uv run python tools/vocab/query.py --excluded title --issue 606`) — 2026-10-04 #606. `pan` alone has since become a `salutation` reading written `default = false` in cs.toml and pl.toml, which a Czech or Polish choice turns on and the default does not read — 2026-10-30 #753, phase 3f.

- 2026-11-10 #1151 — PERSIAN TITLES, AND THE PERSIAN LETTERS OF THE ARABIC ONES.
