# H6 — a title before a family comma

## Standing

- A run of title words opening the part before a family comma is the title, joining the title field in input order with any title after the comma, and the comma decision is untouched, so the reading is the same under every name order: `Dr. Smith, John` → title 'Dr.', family 'Smith'; `Prof. Dr. Brühl, Ada Cornelia` → title 'Prof. Dr.' — 2026-10-17 #701.
- The run admits a word by vocabulary only, never by H2's shape, since the slot is a surname's slot too and a period-marked word there is as often a surname's abbreviated head, a given name written short or a stray period as a title: `Xyz. Smith, John` → family 'Xyz. Smith', `Sta. Maria, Juan` → family 'Sta. Maria', `Mt. Pleasant, Jane` → family 'Mt. Pleasant'. A title missing there is fixed by listing it, a given name written short by a `given` reading with `same_as`, and a stray period reads where the rules put it — 2026-10-17 #701; 2026-10-18 #710; 2026-11-06 #1115.
- The run leaves the family its last piece (`Dr., John` → family 'Dr.'), stops at a word that is a particle too (`St Pierre, Ed` and `Freiherr von Berg, Ed` keep their families, while `Dr. Freiherr von Berg, Ed` peels 'Dr.'), and stops where nothing but post-nominals stands behind a title (`RA PhD Jr., John` → family 'RA'), so a title/suffix dual with a name word behind it peels (`Rev. Sr. Smith, Mary` → title 'Rev. Sr.') — 2026-10-17 #701.
- A surname-like title opening a two-word surname is the title (`Pope Smith, John` → title 'Pope'), an accepted cost — 2026-10-17 #701.
- `gov` is a title (`Lt. Gov. Smith, John` → title 'Lt. Gov.'), the first piece the run leaves is the family whatever it is (`Dr. PhD Smith, John` → family 'PhD Smith'), and the particle stop is all that keeps a rank in the family, so `Herzog Lambsdorff, Otto` reads title 'Herzog' (`Graf Lambsdorff, Otto`, a rank particle since decisions/vocabulary-collisions' 2026-11-04 (Derek), #709 entry, keeps family 'Graf Lambsdorff') — 2026-10-17 #701.
- The comma-less writing of an abbreviated surname is not this rule's; `sta` and `sto` are saint particles, so `Juan Sta. Maria` reads family 'Sta. Maria' and `Juan Sta. Maria Reyes` family 'Sta. Maria Reyes' — 2026-10-18 #710; 2026-11-04 #725.

- 2026-10-17 (Derek), #701 — A TITLE OPENING THE PART BEFORE A FAMILY COMMA IS THE TITLE. `Dr. Smith, John` read family 'Dr. Smith', and every release did the same: 1.4.0 from PyPI gives last 'Dr. Smith' and 'Prof. Brühl' (suffix 'Dr.') for `Prof. Dr. Brühl, Ada Cornelia` (measured 2026-10-17, `PYTHONSAFEPATH=1`, wheel path asserted). The reading was written down on purpose as Accepted clauses of rules/H2 and #H3 ("before a family comma the pre-comma text is wholly the family name") and assumed by C1's Freiherr clause ('Prof. Cruz, Ed', rules/C3's Accepted clause since #728); all three are rewritten here. The listing form moves the surname to the front and leaves the title where it was written, and CLDR's index renderings write exactly that in several locales (`prof. dr. von Selimović Hodžić, Izeta Amra`, `г-н Вернер, Иван Асен`).
  DECIDED (Derek, the five points of the design proposal): (1) VOCABULARY ONLY. H2's shape does not fire before a family comma, so `Xyz. Smith, John` keeps family 'Xyz. Smith' — the slot is a surname's slot too, as the trailing slot is (#H5), and every CLDR case is a listed title. (2) The run leaves the family its last piece (`Dr., John`, `Greve, Anna`). (3) A word that is a particle too stops the run, bare or period-marked, so `St Pierre, Ed` and the 1919 legal-surname reading of `Freiherr von Berg, Ed` (rules/C3's Accepted clause) stand, and a title in front of them still peels. (4) The peeled titles join the title field in input order, and the comma decision (`_comma.decide`) is untouched — the count before the comma already treated a title as no name word. (5) ACCEPTED COST: a bare surname-like title opening a two-word surname is the title, `King Smith, John` → title 'King', as `Greve Anna` already reads (#H1).
