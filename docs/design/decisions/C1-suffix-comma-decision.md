# C1 — the suffix-comma decision

## Standing

- Only the part after the first comma decides, and the listing form stands unless more than one word precedes the comma, so `Smith, PhD` keeps it — 2026-07 PR #288. How the words are counted is decisions/C3's; the exception for a suffix word ending the name before the comma is decisions/C12's.
- The decision consults the suffix vocabulary by definition — 2026-07 plan deviation #3.
- The lenient token test is the default; `lenient_comma_suffixes=False` restores the strict one — 2026-07-12/13 PR #288.
- The wholly-suffix predicate lives in the vocabulary layer, shared with the honorific peel's segment test — 2026-08 #319.
- The comma is decided once, after classify, and a part read as postnominal is bound before any join; a connective surname counts its words, nothing after the decision unsettles what it bound, and a positional read before a suffix comma reports as the comma-less name does — 2026-10-06 #613, decisions (1)–(4) and the costs accepted with them.
- The CJK stage asks the comma decision itself rather than a copy of it — 2026-10-06 #613, the CJK radar tier.
- An empty part before the comma is read by the decision, not left to the listing walk — 2026-10-06 #613, the PR review.
- A bound caps word that carries the name's case contrast keeps its capitals' marks, so case repair leaves it as written (rules/R4) — 2026-10-06 #613, the PR review.
- A generation ending the name before the comma meets paired initials after it this way: dotted, they stay the given name and the generation the suffix (C5); undotted as two capitals, they are the credential (C12); a roman numeral S10 reads stays the suffix too — 2026-10-19 #761; 2026-10-25 #774.

- The Ethiopic comma U+1363 and the Burmese comma U+104A count as the comma, as the Arabic and fullwidth commas do, so `ከበደ፣ አበበ` reads family 'ከበደ', given 'አበበ' — 2026-11-08 #1202
- The N'Ko U+07F8, Mongolian U+1802, Manchu U+1808, Lisu U+A4FE, Vai U+A60D and Bamum U+A6F5 commas count as the comma too, and the Armenian U+055D, which marks an apposition, does not — 2026-11-08 #1202, the follow-up
- A comma between the words of a clause rules/S1 sets apart is any character that counts as the comma, so `(PhD፣ Sr) John Smith` reads title 'Sr', suffix 'PhD' as `(PhD, Sr) John Smith` does — 2026-11-08 #1202, the follow-up
Not standing: 2026-10-04 #611's "the rethinks are filed, not done" — #613 rethought C1 (folding P6 in) and #614 S2, both shipped in 2.4.0, which overtook its "neither for 2.4".
