- 2026-09-06 #436/#437 — the entry boundary is the comma the writer typed, not the shape of the segments. Two consecutive post-nominals are one entry iff they sit in the same comma bucket and nothing between them parts the run; `comma_bucket` is the function `segment` builds segments with, so "same part" here is an identity with segment's answer rather than a resemblance to it (mechanisms.md#ONE-PREDICATE-PER-QUESTION).
