# generational-words — junior and senior in other languages (2026-10-14, #661)

Closes #661. 31 spelled-out generational words join SUFFIX_WORDS, and one predicate moves with them (the run-start bullet below), so `Antonio Pavlović mlađi` reads family Pavlović, suffix mlađi, where 1.4.0 and 2.4.2 read middle Pavlović, family mlađi (checked against both wheels). The class is decided once — a word meaning junior/senior (or the Younger/the Elder) written spaced AFTER the name — and each word was then checked two ways. First, collisions: a suffix claim acts on the trailing word, so the risk is a borne name there, and `tools/name_usage.py` found none (US Census, SSA, Wikidata; nuorempi's one Wikidata bearer of each kind is noise, as the issue said). Second, usage, because the issue's list came partly from ilib's machine-translated test data: each word was found in the trailing spaced position in real titles on its own language's Wikipedia (2026-10-13, e.g. "Hans Holbein mlađi", "Плиний Младший", "Pieter Brueghel vanhempi", "बलबीर सिंह सीनियर"). The words carry `issue = 661` in their language files (`uv run python tools/vocab/query.py --issue 661`, decisions/vocabulary-model). Feminine forms ship beside the masculine wherever the language has them (mlađa, младшая, молодша, jaunesnioji); lt vyresnioji is the grammatical feminine with no titled bearer found, and ships on grammar. ru/uk and cs/sk were not in the issue and were added on the same evidence (Derek, 2026-10-14).

## Standing

- A word meaning junior or senior (the Younger, the Elder) and written spaced AFTER the name is `SUFFIX_WORDS` vocabulary, each word vetted two ways before it ships: no borne name in the trailing position (`tools/name_usage.py`), and attested in that position in real titles on its own language's Wikipedia, except `vyresnioji`, the lt grammatical feminine with no titled bearer found, which ships on grammar; feminine forms ship beside the masculine wherever the language has them — 2026-10-14 #661.
- The words carry `issue = 661` or `issue = 679` in their language files (`uv run python tools/vocab/query.py --issue 661`, `--issue 679`), and their spaced readings are live: `Pieter Brueghel vanhempi` reads suffix 'vanhempi', `Antonio Pavlović mlađi` family 'Pavlović', suffix 'mlađi' — 2026-10-14 #661; 2026-10-14 #679.
- CLDR is corroboration and not a source: its `generation` field holds field-label translations, adjectives written before the name and abbreviations nobody writes after one, so a word is added on its own language's Wikipedia and the collision counts, never because CLDR has it — 2026-10-14 #661; 2026-10-14 #679.
- A refused word carries `excluded = { generation = … }` with its reason in the file of the language whose reading is refused (`uv run python tools/vocab/query.py --excluded generation`) — 2026-10-14 #661; 2026-10-14 #679.
- The words ship spaced only, `二世` excepted, so a hyphen-glued Jr. is no suffix: `Теодор Рузвелт-младши` reads family 'Рузвелт-младши' — 2026-10-14 #661.
- `二世` is a generation word that peels spaced and glued, the one exception to spaced-only shipping: `陳怡君二世` reads family '陳怡君', suffix '二世', and its `ja.toml` and `zh.toml` entries carry `issue = 671` and `attached` with `issue = 707` — 2026-10-21 #762.
- The generational words whose collisions are with OTHER languages written in the same script (fr `père`; the Latin abbreviations `ml.` and `st.`) ship in no default, that being the case a locale pack exists for: `Jiří Dienstbier ml.` reads family 'ml.', and no cs pack exists; cs `st.` is recorded as a reading off by default, for that pack — 2026-10-14 #679; 2026-10-25 #752.
- fr `fils` is a generation reading gated by its family reading, its case and the count (rules/S11): `Alexandre Dumas fils` reads suffix 'fils' and `Marie Fils` family 'Fils', both reported — 2026-10-25 #752.

Elsewhere: the run start that lets any unambiguous suffix word of two or more letters begin the credential run, whatever its script (`Antonio Pavlović mlađi Jones` reads suffix 'mlađi Jones', as does `Pieter Bruegel stariji Jones` with 'stariji Jones'), is decisions/S8's 2026-10-14 (Derek), #679 entry, which retracted this file's #602 run-start bullet; the glued peel of 二世 is decisions/W2's 2026-10-17 #707 entry.

- pt `júnior`, the accented spelling of `junior`, ships beside it with `same_as = "jr"` and inherits its vetting and its accepted collisions rather than being re-vetted under the two tests above (Junior is a borne surname, 2,146 US Census bearers, where `senior` was refused for 3,864): `José Silva Júnior` reads family 'Silva', suffix 'Júnior', as `José Silva Junior` does — 2026-10-23 (Derek), #843.
- The Brazilian agnomens `Filho`, `Neto`, `Netto`, `Sobrinho` and their feminines are generation readings of the `agnomen` kind, gated by the count alone (rules/S11): `Pedro Alves Filho` reads family 'Alves', suffix 'Filho', and `Ana Neto` keeps family 'Neto', both reported — 2026-10-25 #752.

Open: #684 (locale packs for `Jiří Dienstbier ml.` and `st.`, the generational words that collide outside their language); #843 (the agnomens' abbreviations FH, FO, NT and SB).

- **2026-10-23 #843 (Derek) — JÚNIOR SHIPS; THE AGNOMENS WAIT.** "Suffix, Júnior only for now." `júnior` joins `pt.toml` as a generation reading with `same_as = "jr"`: the standard Portuguese spelling, which `José Silva Júnior` read as family 'Júnior', middle 'Silva' in 1.4.0 and 2.5.0 (checked against both wheels) while `José Silva Junior` read suffix 'Junior'. Its collisions are `junior`'s, already accepted: a given name (Wikidata 37+ as Júnior, 43,182+ US SSA births as Junior), where the suffix claim does not act (`Júnior Silva` keeps given 'Júnior'), and family bearers (Wikidata 49+, US Census 2,146 as JUNIOR), which a suffix word costs its surname wherever it ends a name (`Pedro Júnior` reads given 'Pedro', suffix 'Júnior', as `Pedro Junior` does), counts from `tools/name_usage.py` on 2026-10-23. In the local corpus `tse-donors` (fetched 2026-10-22), 1,071 of its 854,531 records write it anywhere in the name (`tools/name_usage.py`), 991 distinct filed names end in it, and 983 records end in it, in either case, where the tax registry's name for the same donor ends `JUNIOR` (last words compared after `str.upper()`). The accented spelling reads as the unaccented one in all 120 pairs of a grid of 20 shapes (comma shapes, an empty head, a title, a particle, a trailing title, a nickname, a maiden clause, a credential and a name word behind it, both one-case spellings) under all three orders, composed and decomposed, comparing fields, ambiguity kinds and `initials()`; with `júnior` removed from the lexicon (`Lexicon.default().remove(suffix_words={'júnior'})`), 108 of the 120 pairs differ, and the 12 that do not hold Júnior where no suffix reading reaches it (`Júnior Silva`, and `Júnior, José`, before a family comma). Measured 2026-10-23 on `tse-donors` against the tree before this change (`snapshot.py --local tse-donors`, baseline recorded with `--tree`): 993 of 848,351 filed names move, 990 ending in Júnior and gaining it as the suffix out of the family name (3 of them two-word names, `RONALDO JÚNIOR`, left with no family as `RONALDO JUNIOR` is), and 3 with Júnior inside the name, where it starts a credential run (decisions/S8) that takes the surnames behind it, `JORGE ANTÔNIO JÚNIOR BARBOSA SILVA` reading family 'ANTÔNIO', suffix 'JÚNIOR BARBOSA SILVA' as the JUNIOR spelling already did; and `local_corpora.py pairs tse-donors --agreement` stops listing JÚNIOR / JUNIOR (866 accents-only pairs read with two surnames before, none after). They are legally part of the surname, and `Neto` is a borne surname and given name alone (Wikidata 276+ family, 12+ given), which a suffix reading would leave with no family, as `Pedro Junior` has none. No exclusion records them, because nothing was refused: the question is open on #843.
