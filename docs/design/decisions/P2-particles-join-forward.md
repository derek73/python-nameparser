## Standing

Examples read under the default order unless a line names another.

- A title that is not also a particle does not displace the leading particle out of the leading position (a title-particle leads, below): `Sir de Mesnil` groups [Sir] [de] [Mesnil], the leading particle chaining nothing (P4), and its family reading is P1's fold; so `Dr. Van Johnson` reads as `Van Johnson` does, given 'Van' — 2026-08 #367.
- The chain stops where the trailing run begins, so a run of the unambiguous suffix vocabulary opening it is the suffix (`John Smith Mc V` → suffix 'Mc V') while one continuing a prefix run is a particle (`John van Mc` → family 'van Mc'); the fork reads the piece before a numeral as it stands (`John van der J. V` → family 'van der J. V'), and a numeral with a suffix behind it stays in the chain (`John van der Berg V Jr` → family 'van der Berg V') — 2026-08-22 #424.
- Where the name begins has one definition, assign's leading-title run, H2's unlisted abbreviations included, so `Xyz. van Johnson` reads as `Dr. van Johnson` does — 2026-08-22 #424.
- The leading position is decided once, as the LAST title in assign's leading-title run, read as written before H3's give-back, that is also a particle, else the first piece past the run: `Freiherr St John Smith MA` → title 'Freiherr St', given 'John', family 'Smith', suffix 'MA' (default order); `Freiherr St van Berg MA` → title 'Freiherr St', family 'van Berg'; `Dr. Mc Mc` → given 'Dr.', suffix 'Mc Mc' (default order) — 2026-10-10 (Derek), #624.
- Assign's re-read fallback, where group's joins leave no name word past the titles, stays, since it guards totality — 2026-10-10 (Derek), #624.
- Particle groups are distributed across the middle and family fields rather than combined, the combined double-surname reading living in the surnames view (`Joao da Silva do Amaral de Souza` → middle 'da Silva do Amaral', family 'de Souza', surnames 'da Silva do Amaral de Souza') — 2026-08-16.
- Suffix pieces are not skipped when the chain looks for the leading piece, since skipping them reads worse: `Ph.D. Van Johnson` → given 'Ph.D.', family 'Van Johnson'; `Dr. Ph. D. Van Johnson` → family 'Van Johnson' — 2026-10-19 #760.

Elsewhere: the stop is S2's trailing run, read once after P3's joins and before the chain is made, counting the chain's own run as the one name word it will make (`John van der Berg V` → family 'van der Berg', suffix 'V'), the re-ask after the chain's merges running only where the run was not read there, and on a main segment the chain stops in front of a trailing title (`John van der Berg Prof.` → title 'Prof.', family 'van der Berg' under the default order) — decisions/S2's 2026-10-07 (Derek), #614 entry and decisions/H5's 2026-10-07 #614 entry, indexed here by this file's 2026-10-07 #614 cross-reference. A particle that is also suffix vocabulary heads the name word behind it before a family comma in any case (`SMITH VD MA, JOHN` → family 'SMITH VD MA') — decisions/P9's 2026-10-02 #573 entry. A bare ambiguous acronym written Title-case in a mixed-case name joins the chain (`John van der Berg Ma` → family 'van der Berg Ma') and one written in capitals ends it with no words to spare (`Freiherr von Berg MA` → family 'von Berg', suffix 'MA') — decisions/S6's 2026-09-14 #289 + #516 and 2026-09-15 (Derek) entries; the absorbed acronym reports `suffix-or-name`, and a word a particle run took (`anh van do`) reports nothing — decisions/S6's 2026-09-18, WHOLE-PR REVIEW ROUND entry; a one-letter numeral behind a title and a chain has no word to spare and joins the chain (`Freiherr von Richthofen V` → family 'von Richthofen V') — decisions/S10's 2026-10-17 (Derek), #718 entry. After a family comma the given part's chain stops where `tail_reading`'s run begins — decisions/H5's 2026-10-10 (Derek), #623 entry.
