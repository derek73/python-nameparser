# R6 — the default rendering follows the written name

## Standing

- The default `str()` and `render()` lay a name out in `ParsedName.order`, the order it was read in, and keep the separator written between two words side by side both as written and as laid out, normalized by kind; a family comma is never kept, and a comma between the family and its suffix is (decisions/vocabulary-model's #754 output-model entry, item 6) — 2026-10-28 #754 (Derek).
- `render(spec: str | None = None)`: `None` is the order-aware default, and a caller's spec renders exactly as written, so passing 2.x's default spec keeps given-first output (decisions/3-0-reevaluations, THE RENDER SIGNATURE) — 2026-10-28 #754 (Derek).
- Two words are side by side as written when they are consecutive in `ParsedName.tokens`; a comma is kept only before a suffix; and credentials written apart keep the suffix field's comma join — 2026-10-29 #754.

Open: how a pack's CLDR-shaped render pattern is reached, (a) through a convention recorded on the name or (b) through the parser (decisions/vocabulary-model's #754 render-patterns entry); this rule reads `order` alone and forecloses neither.

## Log

- 2026-10-28 #754 (Derek) — DECIDED, as item 6 of decisions/vocabulary-model's #754 output-model entry, which holds the reasons and the measurements: the default rendering follows the order a name was read in and keeps its written separators in a normalized form, through `render(spec: str | None = None)`, a family-first layout keeping the title first and the suffix last. This file records the rule that decision made, rules/R6.
- 2026-10-29 #754 — IMPLEMENTED (sub-phase 4e). `ParsedName.order` is the order assign read the name under, `GIVEN_FIRST` after a family comma and the declared `name_order` where no positional read happened; it carries `ADDED_IN = "3.0"`, so a 2.x pickle loads with `None` and renders given-first, and it is left out of `==` and `comparison_key()`. Where the decided text left a choice, the reading taken: two words are side by side as written when they are consecutive in `ParsedName.tokens`, so a marker word the parse dropped, or a field `replace()` spliced in, leaves the pair apart and takes the layout's joiner; credentials written apart keep the suffix field's comma join (`John Smith, MD, PhD` → 'John Smith, MD, PhD') and credentials written side by side keep their space (`John Smith MD PhD` → 'John Smith MD PhD'), as the suffix field already joined them (rules/R1). A comma is kept only before a suffix, where the decided text keeps "one comma the parse reads" between any two words side by side and names the family comma as the one never kept: a comma before anything but a suffix reads back as a family comma, which the rendering may not write. Two shapes reached it. A family comma whose given part went to the family stands between two family words in the same order, so the first build kept it (`smith, de ma` rendered 'smith, de ma'); and titles past a second comma are moved to the front with their comma (`Петренко Анна Марія Богдан-Зиновій, доц., проф.` rendered 'доц., проф. Петренко …', which reads back with family 'доц.'). The alternative, keeping every comma but a family comma, is the literal reading, and costs those two read-backs.
