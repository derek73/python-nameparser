# C15 — a comma with nothing before it

## Standing

- A comma with nothing before it is a family comma naming no surname, the part after it read as the given part after a family comma reads, held by an invariant over two inputs rather than by a sweep shape: `, X` reads as `Smith, X` reads, less the surname, its fields and ambiguity kinds alike; a particle P6 attaches is then the family alone (`, Jane van` → family 'van') — 2026-10-27 #892.
- H1 is the one exception: a title and one name word, or a bound pair standing for it, read as the family (`, Dr. John` → family 'John', `, Dr. abdul Rahman` → family 'abdul Rahman'), the comma naming no surname to decide the field by, as a comma with nothing but titles after it names no given part — 2026-10-27 (Derek).
- No code is written for the empty head alone: P6 attaches behind it as behind any family comma, ahead of H1, so a particle ending the name leaves H1 two name words and the exception does not apply (`, Dr. Jones vd` → given 'Jones', family 'vd') — 2026-10-27 #892 (Derek).

Open: none in this file.
