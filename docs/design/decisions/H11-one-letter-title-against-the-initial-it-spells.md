# H11 — a one-letter title against the initial it spells

## Standing

- A one-letter word written with its period is an initial and not a title where its letter has a capital, whatever title the letter spells, at every reader of the title vocabulary whose answer such a word can move, and so is such a letter cut from a word at its full stops (`U. S. Grant`, `Grant, U.S.`, `U. Smith, Ed`, `Vice U. Smith`, `Dr.U. Smith` and `John (U.) Smith` under `locales.MY` read as the default reads them); written bare it is the title (`U Thein Sein` → title 'U' under the pack); a letter of a script without capitals keeps its title written with its period (`ড. রহিম উদ্দিন` → title 'ড.') — 2026-11-19 #1231.
- The veto is a shape, stated once for every one-letter title a lexicon holds, a caller's included, as rules/P7 states it for the particles, and asked through one predicate, `_lexicon._spells_a_cased_initial`; no per-word property (the reserved `bare_only`) is built for it — 2026-11-19 #1231.
