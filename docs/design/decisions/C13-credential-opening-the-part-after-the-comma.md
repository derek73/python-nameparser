- A credential that is also a given name written short (`md`) opens the given part after a family comma behind no whole name, where its writing says it is the name: alone in the part with no title in front, in a name of mixed case, with a lower-case letter its credential's case mask does not write (`Rahman, Md` → given 'Md'); or with a name word behind it, titles in front or not (`Rahman, Md Abdul`, `Uddin, Mr Md Karim`), a name word having no suffix or title reading of any kind, being no half of a split credential, holding a letter, and in mixed case being no word in capitals but an initial.
