- 2026-11-06 #845 — A FOUR-CHARACTER HAN NAME OPENING WITH TWO SURNAMES IS A SURNAME PAIR BEFORE A TWO-CHARACTER GIVEN NAME. Measured on the `tw-donors` local corpus (Taiwan's political donation reports, fetched 2026-10-22): 4,868 of its four-character Han donor names open with two characters that are each a listed single surname under `locales.ZH` and are not a listed compound, and every one read family 1, given 3 before this change. For 2,076 of them the last three characters are themselves a donor's name in the same file (丁劉碧珠 beside 劉碧珠), the woman's own name before marriage, which is the strongest sign in a corpus with no fields that these are married double surnames (冠夫姓). The same test over the three-character names of that shape gives 1,188 of 51,047, and samples of them are one surname before two (吳子豪, 李元健), which is why the rule counts four characters. Only 114 four-character names that open with a listed surname fall outside the shape and keep 1 + 3, among them transcribed given names (劉以斯帖), and 15 five-character names have it, too few to say whether a compound before a married surname (歐陽李小明) or a pair before a three-character given name is the commoner reading, so five stay out.
