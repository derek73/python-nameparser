# R10 — the second-surname view

## Standing

- `ParsedName.surname2` holds the family's second name unit where rules/O6 read the family as two, and is empty otherwise; it is a view, not a role, so `family`, `surnames`, `as_dict()`, `replace()`'s fields and the repr are unchanged, item 5 of decisions/vocabulary-model's 2026-10-28 #754 output-model entry — 2026-10-29 #754, sub-phase 4d.
- O6's mark is a private tag that assemble drops, carried out on `Reading.second_surname`, a field `Token.reading`'s readings gained and that is not compared, where the RFC had a stable tag: a published tag would make an O6 parse unequal to its own 2.x pickle — 2026-10-29 #754, sub-phase 4d.

Elsewhere: why the second surname is a view of `family` rather than a role, the ORCID measurements behind it, and the `surnames` redefinition declined, decisions/vocabulary-model's 2026-10-28 #754 entry, item 5; how O6 counts a family's words and which names it declines, decisions/O6.

Open: a view for the first unit and its name, `surname` standing close to the `surnames` that exists, with the per-unit particles and base CLDR's `surname-prefix` and `surname-core` would need (the RFC's render-pattern table and Left open); whether a family a connective or a hyphen joins (`Ortega y Gasset`, `García-López`) should read as two units here though O6 counts it as holding two already.

## Log

- 2026-10-29 #754, sub-phase 4d — IMPLEMENTED item 5 of decisions/vocabulary-model's 2026-10-28 #754 entry. What the implementation decided, beyond that entry:
  1. **The mark is published on the reading, not as a stable tag.** The entry and the RFC's freeze table said O6 marks the second unit's start "with a new stable tag" joining `STABLE_TAGS`. `Token` compares its tags, so a tag on a published token makes `Parser(policy=Policy(two_family_names=True)).parse("Gabriel García Márquez")` unequal to the same parse pickled by 2.8.0, which 4b and 4c each kept equal by dropping their kind tags at assemble. So O6 writes `vocab:second-surname`, private, and assemble drops it and sets `Reading.second_surname` on the word's reading, the field added with `ADDED_IN` 3.0.0 and `compare=False` as `Reading.languages` was; `tests/v2/test_types.py` holds the 2.8.0 pickle equal (`parsedname_v28_two_family_names.pickle`). Weighed against: a `ParsedName` field holding the unit's start, which `replace()` would have to keep in step with the tokens it rewrites, where a reading is simply absent from a token `replace()` or `Parser.revise()` writes; and a `ReadingKind` for a second surname's words, which would overwrite the reading a particle or a patronymic there took.
  2. **Every word of the second unit is marked, not only its first.** The view is then a filter over the family's tokens, with no walk from a start and no rule about where the unit ends: the words O3 folds into the family (rendered first) and a particle P6 attached to the first unit are simply unmarked. Given-first, the unit is the family O6 found, before it took the middle word; family-first, it is the unit O6 took after the family. The mark rides the copy that moves the taken unit into the family, so family-first parses pay no frame for it.
  3. **The word keeps the reading it took**, the mark set on it, so a patronymic run in the second unit reads in both views (`Ali Hassan bin Ahmad` gives surname2 and patronymic `bin Ahmad`), as does a word P6's attached patronymic particle makes the parent's name (`Mohamad Ali Mahathir bin` under FAMILY_FIRST). Assemble reads the word as it reads without the mark, so no reading's kind, source or files moves: over 1,296 parses of 36 names under each order, with and without `middle_as_family`, `two_family_names` and the RU and TR_AZ packs, tokens, tags and readings match the base apart from the new field.
  4. **The view stays out of `as_dict()`, `--json`, `comparison_key()` and the repr**, as R8's and R9's do, and is a `render()` key and an assertable field of a rule's example lines, whose grammar now takes a digit in a field name. The snapshot records it where it is not empty (`tools/differential/snapshot.py`'s `SURNAME2_VIEW`), which no row is, since the snapshot parses the default policy; `tools/differential/cldr_agreement.py` scores it on its own beside the merged `family`, as its PENDING_ROLES were written to.
