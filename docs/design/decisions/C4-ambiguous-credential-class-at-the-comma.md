Run membership asks what the word IS, and `vd` is unambiguous suffix vocabulary; company asks whether the word SPEAKS FOR the one behind it, and a particle cannot, P2 joining it forward to exactly that word. Both answers follow from the word's vocabulary, and they differ by design rather than by drift; S2's "end the run" is the company's run, not the part C1 counts. The clause reaches exactly `vd` and `mc`: `do`, also in both vocabularies, is in the ambiguous half and so is already a word of C1's class, reporting where these two do not (`John Smith, Jr do` against `John Smith, Jr vd`).
