# R8 — the generation and credentials views

## Standing

- `ParsedName.generation` and `ParsedName.credentials` partition `suffix` by the reading each word took, a generation or an agnomen in the first and every other word in the second; they are views, not roles, so `suffix`, `as_dict()`, `replace()`'s fields and the repr are unchanged, item 4 of decisions/vocabulary-model's #754 output-model entry — 2026-10-29 #754, sub-phase 4b.
- A listed suffix word's kind is read from the reading index (`_reading_index.suffix_kind_of`, through `_vocab.suffix_kind`), since the compiled sets do not record it — 2026-10-29 #754, sub-phase 4b.

Elsewhere: the field's composition and the 2026-08-16 decision to keep it one field, which still stands, decisions/suffix-field-composition; whether `credentials` should leave the honorifics out, CLDR having no third field, is a value question under the RFC's Phase 4, Left open (docs/design/vocabulary-model-rfc.md).

Open: none in this file.

SUPERSEDES: decisions/A1's 2026-10-26 #752 ACCEPTED clause that a listed suffix word neither a generation by its tag nor an agnomen reads as `post-nominal`, `jr` and `esq` alike. What the implementation decided, beyond that entry:
  1. **The kind of a listed suffix word comes from the reading index.** `jr` and `esq` compile into `suffix_words` with the same tags, so 4a read both as `post-nominal` and pinned the gap with strict xfails. `_vocab.suffix_kind` now asks `_reading_index.suffix_kind_of` for a word tagged `vocab:suffix-word`, a memo whose hit costs no frame, so `Token.reading` and `Ambiguity.alternative` move together: a lone `Jr` names a `generation` alternative where it named a `post-nominal` one, and a credential written as a word (`博士`) reads `credential`. The vocabulary's courtesy, honorific, professional honorific and status kinds have no reading kind of their own and stay `post-nominal`. `_vocab` imports `_reading_index` for it, which tests/v2/test_layering.py's table records.
  2. **The kind is read whatever the reading's default.** `st`, a Czech generation written `default = false`, reads `generation` when a caller's `Lexicon` lists it, while its `languages` name no file, since none holds it on. A pack turning such a reading on (phase 3's `cs`, 3f) then needs nothing more for the kind; a word only a caller's own `Lexicon` lists has no row and reads `post-nominal`.
  3. **Each word is placed by its own reading, and separated as the writer separated it.** A `joined` mark is not only the second word of a split credential (`Ph.` `D.`, which takes the first word's reading) but every word of a suffix run written without commas (`Jr. III PhD`), so a view places each word by its reading. Two words of one view are separated by a comma where the writer typed one anywhere between them and by a space where not, R1's separator, so `MD Jr. PhD` gives credentials 'MD PhD'; the first draft started a new comma-joined entry wherever a word of the other view stood between, writing a comma nobody typed ('MD, PhD'), found by the design-docs review. A connective goes with the word before it (`PhD and MD`, `Jr. and III`), so one joining a generation to a credential dangles at the end of the generation (`Jr. and MD` gives generation 'Jr. and'), accepted rather than given a rule of its own, no corpus name holding the shape.
  4. **A suffix word with no reading is a credential.** `replace()` and `Parser.revise()` write tokens with no `Token.reading` (4a), so `replace(suffix="Jr.")` reads credentials 'Jr.': nothing records what the word was read as, and the catch-all is the view that claims least.
  5. **The views stay out of `as_dict()`, `--json` and the repr**, as `family_base` and the other derived views do: `as_dict()` is the seven fields `replace()` takes back. They are `render()` keys and assertable fields of a rule's example lines.
  6. **The snapshot records them** (`tools/differential/snapshot.py`, a `RECORDED_LATER` pair): `generation` where the suffix holds one, and `credentials` beside it where the suffix holds both, so a row with neither is a suffix of credentials only, or none. No other key moved on recording.
