# C3 — counting the words before the comma

## Standing

- A particle run and the one name word it attaches to are one word in every count of the part before the comma, so a particle surname counts as a one-word surname does: `De La Cruz, Ed` → given 'Ed', family 'De La Cruz'; `van der Berg, MA` → family 'van der Berg', suffix 'MA' — 2026-10-01 #575.
- Where that surname is the whole part, the comma settles P1's fork for a leading particle that could be a given name, under every name order: `Van Buren, Ed` → given 'Ed', family 'Van Buren'; `Van Johnson, Dr.` → title 'Dr.', family 'Van Johnson' — 2026-10-01 #575.
- The particle reaches one word, as P1's fold does, so a word after the surname is a second word and the part keeps the positional read with P1's fork: `van Buren John, Ed` → given 'van', middle 'Buren', family 'John', suffix 'Ed' under the default order — 2026-10-01 #575.
- A bound given-name pair is no one word to the count (`abdul Salam, Ed` → suffix 'Ed'), and a bound given-name word that is a particle too counts as the particle: `Abu Bakar, Ed` → given 'Ed', family 'Abu Bakar'; `Abu Bakar, PhD` → family 'Abu Bakar', suffix 'PhD' — 2026-10-01 #575.
- A word of both the title and the particle vocabulary is no particle where it opens the part and chains inside a surname: `St John, PhD` → title 'St'; `Freiherr von Berg, PhD` → title 'Freiherr'; `de St Pierre, Ed` → given 'Ed', family 'de St Pierre'; and `Freiherr von Berg, Ed` → family 'Freiherr von Berg', given 'Ed' — 2026-10-01 #575.
- A title in front is a word to the count an unambiguous credential takes, so `Dr. van der Berg, PhD` reads as `Dr. van der Berg` does, given 'van' with P1's fork; and behind a one-word particle surname paired initials keep the given name, `De La Cruz, M.J. K.L.` → given 'M.J.', suffix 'K.L.', reporting twice — 2026-10-01 #575.

Elsewhere: the count runs once, in `_comma.decide` over classify's tags, with `name_word_count`'s vocabulary view held equal to them; a connective surname counts its words (`Ortega y Gasset, Dr.` → title 'Dr.', given 'Ortega', middle 'y', family 'Gasset' under the default order; `Ortega y Gasset, Ed` → suffix 'Ed'); and a part before the comma that is one name word with a suffix in it counts as the listing form's one word (`Smith Jr., Esq.` → family 'Smith', suffix 'Jr., Esq.'; `Smith Jr., V` → given 'V', family 'Smith', suffix 'Jr.') — decisions/C1's 2026-10-06 (Derek), #613 entry, its decisions (1) and (4); what keeps `Freiherr` in the family is the particle stop, not the rank's history, so `Graf Lambsdorff, Otto` reads title 'Graf' — decisions/H6's 2026-10-17 (Derek), #701 entry; a given-name title alone after the comma makes a lone surname the given name (`Cruz, Sir` and `Smith Jr., Sir` → given 'Cruz', 'Smith') while a particle surname keeps the family (`van der Berg, Sir` → family 'van der Berg') — decisions/H1's 2026-10-18 #720 entry; a listed title opening the part before a family comma peels (`Prof. Cruz, Ed` → title 'Prof.', given 'Ed', family 'Cruz'), which this file's #575 entry had read as part of the family — decisions/H6's 2026-10-17 #701 entry.
