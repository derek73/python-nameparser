# C11 — what the comma decision reports

## Standing

- A part read wholly as suffixes carries no report that a word of it was read as a name, so a particle chained or an acronym taken into a name is never reported there: `John Smith, Jr., Freiherr von Richthofen` reports only `comma-structure` — 2026-09-28 #544.
- A word of the ambiguous class opening the part after the comma carries the comma's report even where its capitals settle the run (`Doe, MA PhD`, `John Smith, MA MA`, `Smith, MA PhD` each report `suffix-or-name` once), while a capitals-settled run behind another credential is silent (`John Smith, PhD MA`); no `suffix-or-name` report lands past the second comma (`Smith, John, MA`, `John Smith, Jr., MA`) — 2026-10-01.
- The part after a suffix comma reports each word a credential in front of it reads as the run although it may be a name word (`John Smith, PhD van der Berg` → `suffix-or-name` on 'van', 'der' and 'Berg'), and C2's no-name-reading clause means only a word read as a name — 2026-10-11 #641.

Elsewhere: no particle chain is made in such a part at all, which replaced #544's withheld report list — decisions/C7's 2026-10-11 (Derek), #636 entry; `pair_only` asks `is_title_piece` — decisions/C5's 2026-10-19 (Derek), follow-up to PR #738 entry; the listed-member report names the role its word is bound to ("a title" for `John, Si.`, "a credential" for `John, MA`) — decisions/suffix-acronym-collisions' 2026-10-21 (#793) entry and decisions/A1's 2026-10-21 #793 entry; a maiden marker past the second comma being an ordinary word, so `Smith, John, Jr nee Jones MA` reads suffix 'Jr nee Jones MA' where the 2026-10-01 entry read a maiden clause — decisions/M2's 2026-10-04 (Derek), #601 entry.
