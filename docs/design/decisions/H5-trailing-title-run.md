# H5 — the trailing title run

## Standing

- After the trailing suffix run, successive period-marked words the title vocabulary lists chain into the title from the end, leaving one name word standing, and join the title field in input order: `John Smith Prof.` → title 'Prof.', family 'Smith'; `John Smith Prof. Dr.` → title 'Prof. Dr.'; `Dr. John Smith Prof.` → title 'Dr. Prof.' — 2026-09-08 #316.
- The back is claimed by vocabulary and the front by shape, so an unlisted abbreviation (`John Smith Xyz.` → family 'Xyz.') and a bare title word (`John Smith Sir`, `Mary Jane Pope`) are name words there, the walk asking `is_title_piece` and never `is_leading_title` — 2026-09-08 #316. A listed salutation ending the given part after a family comma is the exception, read as the title bare (decisions/H8).
- No fork is reported; where the doubt is real, the word left standing carries it (H4) — 2026-09-08 #316.
- The given part after a family comma gets the walk: `Smith, John Prof.` → title 'Prof.', given 'John' — 2026-09-08 #316.
- The trailing peel and the title chain are one reading run to a fixed point (`_pieces.tail_reading`), so a trailing title is transparent to the suffix reading (`John Smith Jr. Prof.` → suffix 'Jr.'; `abdul rahman MA Prof.` reads as `abdul rahman MA` does, plus the title), and the bound join takes no piece the chain takes (`Sir abdul Prof.` → given 'abdul') — 2026-09-09 (the /simplify round on the bundle's PR).
- Transparency holds for one name word where a title run stands in front of it (`Sir John Prof.` → given 'John'); with none in front, the trailing title decides that word's field as H1 reads it (`Smith Prof.` → family 'Smith', `Smith Sir.` → given 'Smith') — 2026-09-09 (review round 2).
- On a main segment the particle chain stops in front of a trailing title (`John van der Berg Prof.` → title 'Prof.', family 'van der Berg'), and a title a connective joined into a longer unit is no single word (`John Smith Prof. and Dr.` → family 'Prof. and Dr.') — 2026-10-07 #614.
- After a family comma the given part's chain stops where `tail_reading`'s run begins, and P6 reads the particle as though the title were absent: `Smith, John van der Berg Prof.` → middle 'van der Berg', `Smith, Jan van Prof.` → family 'van Smith' — 2026-10-10 #623.
- Where that bound and the given part's own walk disagree behind a title, garbage shapes read off it, recorded and not fixed (`Smith, John Mc Prof. VI` → suffix 'Mc', middle 'Prof. VI') — 2026-10-10 #623.

Elsewhere: a maiden clause in front of a trailing title is no join that hides it (`Mary Smith née Jones Prof.` → title 'Prof.'), decisions/M2's 2026-09-26 #535 entry; a bare ambiguous acronym ending the given part after a family comma is the credential, so `Doe, John Prof. MA` reads title 'Prof.', suffix 'MA', decisions/S5's 2026-09-18 #531 entry on the comma report's reach; the bound join after a family comma declines a title word this run takes (`Berg, abdul Prof.` → title 'Prof.'), decisions/P5's 2026-10-11 #637 entry; a part past the second comma makes no particle chain, so `John Smith, Jr., van der Berg Prof.` reads title 'Prof.', decisions/C7's 2026-10-11 #636 entry; a report names the field its word lands in, closing the `Dr. John of Prince` detail mismatch, decisions/A1's 2026-10-08 #626 entry; the cost of a period behind an ordinary surname that is title vocabulary (`Mary Jane King.` → title 'King.', family 'Jane') is the trailing-position bullet of decisions/vocabulary-exclusions.

- **The doctrine, stated rather than left to be inferred.** A period-marked word is claimed by SHAPE at the front and by VOCABULARY at the back. At the front an unlisted abbreviation is a title (#H2) and the shape outranks the vocabulary, which is why `Esq. Smith` reads title though `esq` is post-nominal vocabulary. At the back there is no shape rule, so only a listed title word chains into the title and `John Smith Xyz.` keeps family `Xyz.`. A BARE title word at the back is a name word — `John Smith Sir` and `Mary Jane King` are untouched — TITLES holding ordinary surnames being exactly why, which is the Excluded (TITLES) block in decisions/vocabulary-exclusions ("the trailing-position rule that must NOT be adopted"). The asymmetry is honest rather than an oversight: #109 shipped the leading inference on purpose, and no blanket trailing vocabulary rule is safe while king, judge and bishop are titles.
