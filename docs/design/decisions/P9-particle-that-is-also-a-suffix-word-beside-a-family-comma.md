# P9 — a particle that is also a suffix word, beside a family comma

## Standing

- A particle that is also an unambiguous suffix word, with a name piece behind it in the part before a family comma, heads that name and stays family in any case (`SMITH VD MA, JOHN`) — 2026-10-02 #573.
- Such a word standing between post-nominals after a family comma keeps the post-nominal reading in any case and reports the fork it decided (`suffix-or-name`); with nothing behind it, it ends the name and attaches by P6 — 2026-10-02 #573.
- Before a family comma, a one-letter numeral's words to spare are counted without the part's particles, wherever they stand: a piece made wholly of particles is no word, so `SMITH VD V, JOHN` reads as `SMITH V, JOHN` and `de Mesnil V, Jean` keeps family 'de Mesnil V'. The count is rules/S10's, stated there and in rules/C1's Precedence, and decisions/S10 carries its Standing line; the record is this file's — 2026-11-05 #1080.
- A never-given particle opening the given part makes the member behind it a name rather than a credential. This is rules/S5's site, indexed here because this entry is its record (decisions/S5's #573 entry points back here) — 2026-10-02 #573.

Open: none in this file. The mixed-case `Doe, Jane PhD vd Ma`, which #573 left aside, was decided silent the same day (decisions/S5's #578) and that was reversed by #602 (decisions/S8's 2026-10-04 entry): it reads suffix 'PhD vd Ma'.
