## Standing

Examples read under the default order unless a line names another.

- A connective-joined run is ONE name word wherever a rule counts name words, H1 and P1's family-first stop included, except C3's count before a comma (Elsewhere, below): `Mr. Jack and Jill` → family 'Jack and Jill' where `Mr. Jack Jill` reads given 'Jack'; `de la Vega y Santos Juan` family-first → family 'de la Vega y Santos' — 2026-08-17.
- The three-word carve-out counts the name's own words, once the maiden name has left: `juan y garcia nee jones` → middle 'y', family 'garcia', maiden 'jones' — 2026-08-21 #418.
- `i` is in `conjunctions` and `conjunctions_ambiguous` together, so `Josep Carod i Rovira` and `Carod i Rovira, Josep` read family 'Carod i Rovira'; the off switch is `Lexicon.default().remove(conjunctions={"i"})`, or deleting `i` from `C.conjunctions`, and a name's fields and repair equal the off switch's but for the three exemptions the entry names (a join between two name words, a disagreement about the letter being an initial, a connective placed among the name words in repair) — 2026-09-20 #397.
- A single-letter connective counts as a name word toward the three-word carve-out wherever it is placed to join, whatever else the vocabulary says it is — 2026-09-20 #397.
- A connective that is also generational vocabulary joins only where a name word stands on each side of it, looked for past any run of connectives and between assign's leading-title run and the trailing run H5's reading gives: `Josep Lluis Carod i III` → suffix 'i III'; `John Quincy Adams i MA Prof.` → family 'Adams', suffix 'i MA', title 'Prof.'. The class is a vocabulary test, not a length test, and the three-word carve-out alone stays single-letter — 2026-09-20 #397.
- The both-sides question is answered once per run of connectives, both sides in one call — 2026-09-21 #397 follow-up.
- Accepted with that scope: a link with nothing on its right joins nothing (`Carod i` → given 'Carod', suffix 'i'); a connective run joins the whole name (`Henry i of England` → given 'Henry i of England'); a lowercase `i` between two name words of a mixed-case name joins silently (`John Paul i Smith` → family 'Paul i Smith'); a particle is a name word for the test (`Josep Carod i de Rovira` → middle 'Carod i de', family 'Rovira'); and in a one-case name `i` is P8's initial and reports (`JOHN I SMITH` → middle 'I', `conjunction-or-initial`) — 2026-09-20 #397.
- `y` stays out of `conjunctions_ambiguous` beside the two marked letters — 2026-09-20 #397.
- The comma decision joins title words with group's connective loop, `_pieces.join_connectives`, so a connective run merges in the comma part (`John Smith, Minister of the Interior and Mrs.` → title 'Minister of the Interior and Mrs.'), a lone single-letter connective there is left to P3's count (`Juan Garcia Lopez, Sr. y Sra.` → given 'y', title 'Sr. Sra.'), and every connective of the generational class is frozen there, a neighbour's join still able to take it in (`Smith, Dr. and i` → title 'Dr. and i') — 2026-10-06 #617.
- With the off switch a one-case `i` is the initial its capital is (rules/S10), so `rovira, i` and `john smith i jr` read alike with it on and off; and a connective letter ending its part is not reported — 2026-10-18, #721.
- P3 keeps the join and its three-word count, and the single-letter connective read as an initial is rules/P8's, the entries deciding it having moved to decisions/P8 — 2026-10-19.
- A frozen link joins nothing of its own but a neighbour's join can absorb it: `Josep Carod Rovira Puig y i` → family 'Puig y i' — 2026-10-19 #760.

Elsewhere: P3's joins are made before S2's trailing run is read, so the run counts a joined part as one name word (`Juan y Garcia X.Y.Z.` → family 'X.Y.Z.') and a salutation keeps its connective (`Mr. and Mrs.` → title 'Mr. and Mrs.') — decisions/S2's 2026-10-07 (Derek), #614 entry, indexed here by the 2026-10-07 #614 cross-reference.
