# W6 — the glued-title peel

## Standing

- A listed title marked `attached = { side = "before" }` glued to the start of the name's first word, before the comma or after a family comma, splits off once, the longest first, and reads as the title (`นายสมชาย ใจดี` → title 'นาย', given 'สมชาย', where 1.4.0 through 3.0.1 read given 'นายสมชาย'); a title listed whole keeps its reading (`นายแพทย์`); where the title would leave a Thai vowel written after its consonant or a mark, the next shorter is tried (`นางสาวิตรี` → title 'นาง', given 'สาวิตรี'), and none peels where none is left (`นางิ`, `นางาเซะ`), nor where any other character that is no letter follows the title (`นาย-สมชาย`, `นางสาว-สุดา`, `นางสาวๆ`) — 2026-11-10 (Derek), #317.
- The peel words are the Thai salutations `นาย`, `นาง` and `นางสาว`, listed by #317, and the spelled-out academic and professional titles #1166 listed with `ว่าที่ร้อยตรี` and its feminine `ว่าที่ร้อยตรีหญิง`; the short forms (`ดร`, `นพ`, `ศ`, `พญ`, `ทพ`), titles listed but not marked, and `คุณ`, which no file lists, never peel glued, each opening real given names (`นพพร`, `ศิรินภา`, `คุณากร`); a title that may be a given name never peels, a caller's included — 2026-11-10 (Derek), #317.
- Every Thai title reading in th.toml addresses the given name (`addresses = "given"`), so a title and one name word reads the word as the given name, glued or written apart, as a given-name title does under rules/H1 (`ดร. สุดา` → given 'สุดา', where 1.4.0 through 3.0.1 read family 'สุดา'; `นาย สมชาย` and `นายสมชาย` → given 'สมชาย', which master read family since #317 listed `นาย`): Thai addresses a person by the given name after any title — 2026-11-10 (Derek), #317's follow-up.

Open: [#1319](https://github.com/derek73/python-nameparser/issues/1319), the dotted Thai ranks and the dotted salutations (`น.ส.`, `ด.ช.`, `ด.ญ.`), which are runs of letters this peel does not read.

## Log

- 2026-11-10 (Derek), #317's follow-up — A THAI TITLE ADDRESSES THE GIVEN NAME. The #317 entry below left open whether a Thai title with one name word behind it reads that word as the given name: rules/H1 reads a title and one name word as the family, save a given-name title, and Thai addresses a person by the given name after a title (คุณสมชาย, อาจารย์สมชาย, ดร.สมชาย), the family name being used little in address. So every title reading in th.toml, the salutations, the academic and professional titles and the ranks, takes `addresses = "given"`. Swept over 22 shapes under the three orders against master 7ebd855f: what moves is exactly a Thai title run and one name word, glued or apart, behind an empty comma head and beside a suffix, nickname or maiden name (`นาง สุดา née ใจดี` → given 'สุดา'); a full name, a family comma and a run whose last title is not Thai (`นาย Dr. สมชาย`, H1's run reading its last word) read as before. MEASURED against master 7ebd855f with `snapshot.py --local <corpus> --base <master checkout>` and `local_agreement.py orcid-researchers --base <master checkout>`: orcid-researchers moves 1 name, `อาจารย์ ป๊อด` → given 'ป๊อด' (family before), its field agreement unchanged; jrc-names reads as the base; the committed snapshot moves W6's own `นายสมชาย` example alone.
- 2026-11-10 (Derek), #317 — A LISTED TITLE GLUED TO THE START OF THE NAME SPLITS OFF. #317 was filed as the mirror of #308's trailing peel (rules/W2): Thai writes `นาย`, `นาง` and `นางสาว` glued to the given name, which read as part of it (`นายสมชาย ใจดี` → given 'นายสมชาย'). The issue asked whether the leading position, where a surname leads in other scripts, could be vetted word by word as W2's tails were; measured, it can.
  THE BAR, by word: over 12,880 distinct Thai given names (PyThaiNLP's male and female lists, Apache-2.0, and the 14,098 candidates of Thailand's 2019 general election in ELECT's dataset, github.com/electinth/dataset-election-62-candidates, read 2026-11-10), none opens with `นาง`, `นางสาว` or any spelled-out title, and one with `นาย` (`นายณัฐ`, in the female list, the salutation typed in). The short forms fail it: `นพ` opens 33 given names (`นพดล`, `นพพร`), `คุณ` 12 (`คุณากร`), `ดร` 4, and `ศ` opens many (`ศิรินภา`), so none of them is marked. Thai Wikipedia's people index (46,211 names) opens a name with `นาย` 17 times and `นาง` 26, the honorific or a noble rank where it is Thai (`นางวิสาขา`, `นายขนมต้ม`, `นายจำนงราชกิจ`), and Japanese transcriptions otherwise (`นางิ`, Nagi; `นางาเซะ`, Nagase), which the opening-letter guard keeps whole.
  THE WRITING: ORCID's given-name boxes from Thailand hold `นาย` glued 23 times and apart 2, `นางสาว` 8 and 2, `นาง` 4, and spelled-out titles glued 4 (`อาจารย์สรศักดิ์`, `ผู้ช่วยศาสตราจารย์สังวาลย์`, `ว่าที่ร้อยตรีหญิงพิราภรณ์`); the 2019 candidates, whose title is a box of its own, are `นาย` 10,059, `นางสาว` 1,676, `นาง` 1,428 and ranks the rest.
  MECHANISM: a title reading takes `attached = { side = "before" }`, the side the build had reserved, compiled into `GLUED_TITLES` (`Lexicon._sets.glued_titles`); the peel runs in the script-segment stage before W2's, licensed by the vocabulary alone as W2's is, so no script range gates it, the ASCII bail above both standing. The site is the first word of the name and, after a family comma, of the given part, past titles written apart (`รองศาสตราจารย์ นายแพทย์สมชาย`); a C-level prefix test keeps a name holding no word that opens with a glued title from paying the frame.
  MEASURED against master ed7dfb8b with `snapshot.py --local <corpus> --base <master checkout>` and `local_agreement.py orcid-researchers --base <master checkout>`: orcid-researchers moves 7 names, each gaining its title and keeping the given name (`นายวัชระ ใบยูซบ` → title 'นาย', given 'วัชระ'; `นางสาวภารดี ศรีศิริ`, `นางจารุกัญญ์ พริกบุญจันทร์`, `นายแพทย์สุรินทร์ ธนพิพัฒนศิริ`, `อาจารย์สุขรักษ์ แซ่เจี่ย`), all 7 of them field-agreement regressions only because the filer typed the title into the given-name box; jrc-names reads as the base. No other local corpus holds Thai names, and only a word opening with a glued title can move, so none other was run. The committed snapshot moves no corpus name; it gains W6's examples.
  THE REVIEW, 2026-11-10: the site reaches the family name too, before a family comma (`นายใจดี, สมชาย` → title 'นาย', family 'ใจดี') and under a family-first order, where the first word is the family, so surnames were vetted as given names were: over 12,073 Thai surnames (PyThaiNLP's list and the 2019 candidates' surname box), one opens with `นาย` (`นายกชน`) and none with another marked word. A remainder still glued at a full stop was left whole, T8's division having run on the word whole first, and S3's any-title-chunk reading took it, given name and all (`อาจารย์ดร.สมชาย ใจดี` → title 'อาจารย์ดร.สมชาย', where master read given); the peel now divides it as T8 would before any comma, and after a family comma it stays whole as T8's Except has it, the given part then empty (`ใจดี, อาจารย์ดร.สมชาย`, as `ใจดี, ดร.สมชาย` reads, #1311). A caller's title marked `attached` that also holds a given reading compiled into the peel set and split the word without reading a title (`คุณสมชาย` → given 'คุณ'); a title that may be a given name is now left out of it, being a title only in a phrase (rules/H9). `Lexicon.add` refuses `attached = "before"` on a phrase, as the build does.
  THE DOCS REVIEW, 2026-11-10: the peel took the longest title only, so where it left a mark (`นางสาวิตรี`, Mrs. Sawitri, a common given name) the word stayed whole, while `นาง สาวิตรี` read the title; it now falls back to the next shorter marked title. The given names vetted were those opening WITH a title, not those opening with the letters a longer title adds past a shorter one (`สาว…` behind `นาง`, `แพทย์…` behind `นาย`, `หญิง…` behind the feminine titles), which the fallback reads only where a vowel or a mark follows the longer title (`นางสาวิตรี` as `นาง สาวิตรี`); a given name opening with the longer title's letters and a consonant reads the longer (`นางสาวน้อย` → title 'นางสาว', given 'น้อย', where `นาง สาวน้อย` reads given 'สาวน้อย'), the writer's spacing being the only signal there. The site walk passed over a title listed whole but not one joined at its full stops (`รศ.ดร. นายแพทย์สมชาย` kept given 'นายแพทย์สมชาย'); it passes over both now, though not a title by H2's shape alone.
