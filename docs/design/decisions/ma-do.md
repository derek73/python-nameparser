# ma-do — ambiguous acronyms by decision

## Standing

- `ma` and `do` are members of the ambiguous acronym set (`suffix_acronyms_ambiguous`: acronym readings on a word with a `given` or `family` reading, decisions/vocabulary-model 2026-10-25, `uv run python tools/vocab/query.py --word ma`), kept there because both are common surnames; the set has grown past these two since (decisions/suffix-acronym-collisions), and the marking is still what lets `Jack Ma` read family 'Ma' with `suffix-or-name` reported, while `John Smith MA` reads suffix — 2026-07-17.
- In a name written in more than one case, a member in capitals leans credential and is taken with nothing to spare (`Jack MA`, `Jack DO` → given 'Jack', suffix 'MA'/'DO'), where `Jack Ma` keeps family 'Ma'; a deliberate break from v1, which read `Jack MA` as family 'MA' — 2026-09-15 #289.
- A member written with its periods, one after each letter, counts unambiguously (`Jack M.A.`, `Jack D.O.` → suffix, `given-or-family` reported when only the given name remains), and one trailing period is no such spelling (`Jack Ma.` → family 'Ma.') — 2026-07-17.
- A parenthesized bare `(MA)` or `(DO)` reads as a nickname, no longer escaping to suffix as in 1.x, and reports `suffix-or-nickname` — 2026-07-17.
- After a family comma `do` is spelling-dependent, being the one member of the set that is also particle vocabulary: `Doe, John DO` reads suffix `DO`, while `Doe, John Do`, `Doe, John do` and `DOE, JOHN DO` attach it to the family by P6 (`Doe, John Do` → family 'Do Doe', `particle-or-given` reported); `ma` is no particle, so `Smith, John Ma` keeps middle 'Ma' and `Smith, John MA` reads suffix — 2026-09-18 #531. Comma-less under `FAMILY_FIRST`, only the Title-case spelling attaches (`Doe John Do` → given 'John', family 'Do Doe', with `particle-or-given` besides `suffix-or-name`), while `Doe John do` and `DOE JOHN DO` read suffix, so the comma and the comma-less writing disagree for `do` (rules/P6, decisions/two-input-invariants).

Elsewhere: the rest of the case reading — Title-case `Ma` and `Do` leaning surname, an all-lower member or a one-case name taking the positional count (`ANH DO` → family 'DO', `JOHN SMITH MA` → suffix) — is decisions/S6's 2026-09-14 #289 + #516 entry and decisions/S4's 2026-09-15 (spec review) entry, and two shapes stand outside the case reading altogether: a particle run in front takes `do` whatever its case (`Anh van DO` → family 'van DO', reporting nothing; rules/P2, decisions/S5's 2026-09-18 (Derek), #531 — CAPITALS DECIDE FOR `do` entry), and an unambiguous credential in front of a member speaks for it (`John Smith PhD Ma` → suffix 'PhD Ma'; decisions/S7's 2026-09-27 (Derek), #544 — A CREDENTIAL RUN KEEPS ITS AMBIGUOUS MEMBERS entry); the count that goes first at the first slot after a comma, a suffix comma included (`Smith, MA` → suffix, `Smith, Ma` → given, `John Smith, Ma` → suffix), is decisions/S6's 2026-09-14 (Derek) and 2026-10-20 (Derek), #761 entries; the pairing that gives `do` its capitals-only credential reading after a family comma, and its accepted cost, is decisions/S5's 2026-09-18 (Derek), #531 — CAPITALS DECIDE FOR `do` entry; the escape that reads a bracketed member as a nickname is decisions/suffix-acronym-collisions' S1 line; the periods gate is rules/S3's since 2026-10-19; whether a word earns the ambiguous marking is decisions/vocabulary-collisions' C-i and decisions/suffix-acronym-collisions', with `do`'s departure from TITLES recorded in the former and further credentials marked ambiguous in the latter (2026-10-21 #796).

Elsewhere: after the name, a member whose writing settles it is the suffix, not the nickname (`John Smith (MA)`, `John Smith (M.A)`; `JOHN SMITH (MA)` and `John Smith (Ma)` keep the nickname) — decisions/S13's 2026-10-26 #810 entry, which supersedes the line above in part.
