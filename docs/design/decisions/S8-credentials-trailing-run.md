# S8 — the credential's trailing run

## Standing

Examples name each field as the default order reads it; under the family-first orders the given and family names trade places.

- An unambiguous suffix word of two or more letters, in any script, standing after two name words with no comma or before a suffix comma, or after the given word in the part after a family comma, starts a run to the end of its part: every later word reads as a suffix except a title word, which reads as a title, and an absorbed name word reports `suffix-or-name` (`John Smith PhD Jones` → suffix 'PhD Jones'; `Eric H. Holder Jr. Attorney General` → suffix 'Jr.', title 'Attorney General') — 2026-10-04 #602; 2026-10-14 #679.
- A roman numeral of two or more letters that reads as the generation starts the run as a listed one does (`John Smith VI Jones` → suffix 'VI Jones', as `John Smith III Jones`), its shape alone reported — 2026-10-25 #784.
- What never starts the run: a title word (`Mary Jane Pope Smith` keeps family 'Smith'), a member of the ambiguous class but a credential split across a space (below) and a listed member its capitals settle (below), a single letter, a connective (`Josep Carod i Rovira` keeps family 'Carod i Rovira'), and a word that is also particle or bound given-name vocabulary (`Mohamed Ali Abd Allah` → family 'Allah'); a title/suffix dual does start it — 2026-10-04 #602.
- An honorific or generational word starts the run as a credential does, whatever its script (`राम शर्मा साहब मोहन` → suffix 'साहब मोहन'); the one-character honorifics stay out by the two-letter minimum — 2026-10-14 #679.
- A lone particle counts toward the two name words only where the credential stands right behind it (`John von PhD Jones` → family 'von', suffix 'PhD Jones', under the family-first orders given 'von'); a particle run counts once, and a particle the run took is not asked (`Freiherr von vd PhD Jones` keeps middle 'PhD') — 2026-10-10 #627.
- Lifting the credential out of the name, the family-comma path's older reading (`John Smith PhD Jones` as family 'Jones', suffix 'PhD'), was declined — 2026-10-04 #602.
- An ambiguous acronym written with its periods starts the run (`John Smith M.A. Jones` → suffix 'M.A. Jones'); and so, since 2026-10-25 #785, does a listed member written in capitals in a name of mixed case, its writing settling it, after the given word after a family comma (`Smith, John MA Jones` → suffix 'MA Jones') and elsewhere only where no name word stands behind it (`John Smith MA JD Jones` → suffix 'MA JD Jones', while `John Smith MA Jones` keeps family 'Jones', 2026-10-26 (Derek)); `John Smith Ma Jones` and `JOHN SMITH MA JONES` keep family 'Jones', and `John Smith DO Jones` family 'DO Jones', its particle reading refusing the start — 2026-10-20 #761; 2026-10-25 #785; 2026-10-26 (Derek), #785.
