# O2 — Turkic rotation

## Standing

- Under Turkic handling a name of exactly four name words ending in a standalone marker reads family-first, at v1 parity: `Ali Ahmad Vali oglu` under `locales.TR_AZ` → family 'Ali', given 'Ahmad', middle 'Vali oglu'; any other count keeps the positional reading, the marker standing in a name field (`Ali Ahmad oglu` → family 'oglu') — 2026-07-02 PR #288.
- The marker patterns are NFC literals with `qızı` a literal alternative, dotless ı not case-folding to i, so all-caps `QIZI` falls to the ASCII alternative and decomposed input does not match (`Ali Ahmad Vali oğlu` typed decomposed → family 'oğlu'); suffix-attached Kazakh and Uzbek patronymics (`Әбішұлы`) are out of scope — 2026-07-02.
- The rotation stands down under a declared family-first order by O1's gate on the order assign read: `oglu Ahmad Vali Ali` under FAMILY_FIRST reads family 'oglu' as plain FAMILY_FIRST does, and `Ali Ahmad Vali oglu` under FAMILY_FIRST_GIVEN_LAST reads given 'oglu', middle 'Ahmad Vali' — 2026-09-07 #384.
- The Uzbek marker `o'g'li` is matched with any of six apostrophes between its letters: the typed `'`, U+2019, U+02BB (the official orthography's `ʻ`), U+2018, U+02BC and the backtick, so `Sa'dullayev Alisher Zafar o‘g‘li` under `locales.TR_AZ` reads family 'Sa'dullayev' — 2026-11-08 #1186.
- Re-deriving the order from the marker under a declared family-first order, and reporting where the marker and the declared order disagree, are declined for decisions/O1's reasons — 2026-09-07 #384.

Not standing: 2026-08-15, the same open interaction question as decisions/O1's, answered by 2026-09-07 #384.

Open: none in this file.

Uzbek text writes the marker's `ʻ` with whatever the keyboard gives: U+2018 is a word processor's smart quote for a typed `'` after a letter, U+02BC the modifier apostrophe, the backtick a common ASCII stand-in. Before it, each of the three left the marker a plain name word: `Otabek Sobirjon o‘g‘li Musurmonov` read patronymic '' where the typed spelling reads 'Sobirjon o'g'li' (R9), and the family-first `Sa'dullayev Alisher Zafar o‘g‘li` read family 'o‘g‘li' where its Cyrillic twin `Саъдуллаев Алишер Зафар угли` rotates (measured on master 51d36a31). The issue measured (2026-11-08) Uzbekistan's Legislative Chamber and Senate in OpenSanctions (`uz_legislative_chamber`, `uz_senate`, 205 members): 7 Latin names write the marker with U+2018 and 1 with `'`, and all 7 lost the patronymic, the 2 family-first ones reading family 'o‘g‘li'. The other places an apostrophe class is spelled out were read and left as they are: the nickname delimiters keep only the straight apostrophe word-internal (rules/N2, deliberate since #273), and the name match folds `'`, U+2019 and U+02BC away but not U+02BB, U+2018 or the backtick.
