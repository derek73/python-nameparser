# O2 — Turkic rotation

## Standing

- Under Turkic handling a name of exactly four name words ending in a standalone marker reads family-first, at v1 parity: `Ali Ahmad Vali oglu` under `locales.TR_AZ` → family 'Ali', given 'Ahmad', middle 'Vali oglu' — 2026-07-02 PR #288.
- A name of exactly three name words ending in one reads the father's name and the marker together as the family, the patronymic view holding the same two words: `Бекмырза Чолпонбай уулу` under `locales.TR_AZ` → given 'Бекмырза', family 'Чолпонбай уулу'; two name words and five or more keep the positional reading, the marker standing in a name field (`Cavad oğlu` → family 'oğlu') — 2026-11-12 #1187.
- The marker patterns are NFC literals with `qızı` a literal alternative, dotless ı not case-folding to i, so all-caps `QIZI` falls to the ASCII alternative; decomposed input matches, the word read NFC-composed (decisions/nfd-shapes, #1316); suffix-attached Kazakh and Uzbek patronymics (`Әбішұлы`) are out of scope — 2026-07-02.
- The rotation stands down under a declared family-first order by O1's gate on the order assign read: `oglu Ahmad Vali Ali` under FAMILY_FIRST reads family 'oglu' as plain FAMILY_FIRST does, and `Ali Ahmad Vali oglu` under FAMILY_FIRST_GIVEN_LAST reads given 'oglu', middle 'Ahmad Vali' — 2026-09-07 #384.
- The Uzbek marker `o'g'li` is matched with any of six apostrophes between its letters: the typed `'`, U+2019, U+02BB (the official orthography's `ʻ`), U+2018, U+02BC and the backtick, so `Sa'dullayev Alisher Zafar o‘g‘li` under `locales.TR_AZ` reads family 'Sa'dullayev' — 2026-11-08 #1186.
- Re-deriving the order from the marker under a declared family-first order, and reporting where the marker and the declared order disagree, are declined for decisions/O1's reasons — 2026-09-07 #384.

Not standing: 2026-08-15, the same open interaction question as decisions/O1's, answered by 2026-09-07 #384.

Open: none in this file.
