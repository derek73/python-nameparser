# T8 — a word glued to the next at its full stop

## Standing

- A word of the part before any comma divides at its full stop where a chunk the parser reads on its own meets a name, and reads as it does with a space there (`Dr.John Smith` → title 'Dr.', given 'John'; `Ц.Сандаг-Очир` → given 'Ц.', family 'Сандаг-Очир'): decisions/period-joined-words' target, with the name test it left open answered by position — 2026-11-10 (Derek), #1230, #1235.
- The name after the stop is a word of letters, apostrophes and hyphens that does not open in lower case, is not closed by a full stop of its own nor followed by another chunk, and is no unambiguous title or credential; a word holding a delimiter or a digit, a word the vocabulary reads whole and a saint particle's chunk are not divided — 2026-11-10 #1230, #1235.

- A degree written as a title and its field's initial is listed with its stops and read whole, by default: Cuban `Dr.C.` and `Dra.C.` (Doctor and Doctora en Ciencias) in es.toml, Czech `Mgr.A.` and `Mg.A.` (MgA) in cs.toml; a title and any other letter still divides (`Dr.A. Supriya`) — 2026-11-10 (Derek), #1305.
Elsewhere: which words divide at all, and what a divided word reads as, decisions/period-joined-words' 2026-11-09 #1255 entries; a run of single letters, which stays one word, decisions/T7.

Open: [#1071](https://github.com/derek73/python-nameparser/issues/1071), a saint particle glued to its name (`Mark Jhon Sta.Ana`), which this division leaves whole.
Open: [#1306](https://github.com/derek73/python-nameparser/issues/1306), which field a Mongolian glued initial takes under the default order or a Mongolian choice (moved from #1235, which this division closed): the pack question the spaced `Ц. Сандаг-Очир` already raises.
Open: a chunk in a script whose letters carry spacing vowel signs (`डा.राम शर्मा` keeps title 'डा.राम', where `डा. राम शर्मा` reads title 'डा.', given 'राम'): the shape's letter class counts no spacing mark (decisions/nfd-shapes), so no chunk is found there, moved here from decisions/period-joined-words' Open line.
Open: a word glued at a full stop other than the ASCII one (`Dr．John Smith`, the fullwidth stop decisions/cjk-full-stops reads as a full stop elsewhere), which the division does not read.
Open: a glued word after a family comma, the given part the comma names (`SMITH, J.MICHAEL`), which the division would reach only once the comma's decision is asked first.
