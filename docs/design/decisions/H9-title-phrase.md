# H9 — the title phrase

## Standing

- A title that may be a given name is a title only inside a title phrase, and elsewhere reads as a word the vocabulary does not list, reported `title-or-name` where it is the first name word after any titles: `Dean Smith` reads given 'Dean', `Dean of Students Jane Smith` title 'Dean of Students' — 2026-10-25 #752.
- Its partner is a modifier or a style before it, a title before it that attaches to it or a title after it that it attaches to (`attaches`), or `of` after it; a salutation is never one, nor any other title before it (#853's `Mr Dean Smith`), nor a title after it it does not attach to (`SMITH, DEAN MR.`) — 2026-10-25 #752.
- A modifier in the leading run of titles before the first comma is a title only with a head title after it in that run, `of` after it, or in the object of `of` or `the` there; outside that run (ending the name, H5's, or after a comma) it reads as any title does, the given part after a family comma included (`Lau, Wing` → title 'Wing') — 2026-10-25 #752.
- A title that may be a given name which a caller lists in `titles` is a title wherever it stands, which keeps the 1.4 recipe `c.titles.add("dean")`; a v1 caller who empties `titles` turns the phrase reading off with them, while one who replaces the list keeps it (`c.titles.clear(); c.titles.add('dr')` still reads `Dean of Students` as the title), v1 holding no knob for the set — 2026-10-25 #752.
