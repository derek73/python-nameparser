# H9 — the title phrase

## Standing

- A title that may be a given name is a title only inside a title phrase, and elsewhere reads as a word the vocabulary does not list: `Dean Smith` reads given 'Dean', `Dean of Students Jane Smith` title 'Dean of Students' — 2026-10-25 #752.
- The fork is reported `title-or-name` where the word is the first name word after any titles and no comma has marked the given name: `Dean Smith` and `Dean Smith, MD` report, `SMITH, DEAN MR.` does not — 2026-10-26 #752, phase 2i (Derek). Only where a phrase the vocabulary lists holds the word: `Grace Smith` reports nothing, `Marshal Yanda` does — 2026-10-26 #752, phase 2i, the report's reach (Derek).
- `grace` (a style) and `marshal` (a rank) are titles that may be given names, `sergeant` attaches to the word `major` and `major` to `general` (`attaches` naming a word where a kind would reach too far): `His Grace John Smith`, `Field Marshal Bernard Montgomery` and `Sergeant Major John Smith` read the title; `Mr Justice Smith` keeps given 'Justice', no exception behind a salutation — 2026-10-26 #752, phase 2i (Derek).
- Its partner is a modifier or a style before it, a title before it that attaches to it or a title after it that it attaches to (`attaches`), or `of` after it; a salutation is never one, nor any other title before it (#853's `Mr Dean Smith`), nor a title after it it does not attach to (`SMITH, DEAN MR.`) — 2026-10-25 #752.
- A modifier in the leading run of titles before the first comma is a title only with a head title after it in that run, `of` after it, or in the object of `of` or `the` there; outside that run (ending the name, H5's, or after a comma) it reads as any title does, the given part after a family comma included — 2026-10-25 #752; save a modifier that may be a given name, which the run's test reads in every part (`Lau, Wing Kai` → given 'Wing', `John Smith Wing.` → family 'Wing.') — 2026-10-26 #752, phase 2j, `wing`.
- A title that may be a given name which a caller lists in `titles` is a title wherever it stands, which keeps the 1.4 recipe `c.titles.add("dean")`; a v1 caller who empties `titles` turns the phrase reading off with them, while one who replaces the list keeps it (`c.titles.clear(); c.titles.add('dr')` still reads `Dean of Students` as the title), v1 holding no knob for the set — 2026-10-25 #752. A title 2.6.0 listed that a later batch made a title only inside a phrase stays in v1's default `titles`, read by the phrase while it is there and as no title once removed, so `c.titles.remove('king')` works as in 2.6.0; listing it in v1 cannot make it a title everywhere, which takes `Lexicon.default().add(titles={'king'})` — 2026-10-26 #752, the v1 title list.
